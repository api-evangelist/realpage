Rent is typically the largest recurring expense in a renter's monthly budget, but pay schedules, unexpected expenses and a single monthly due date do not always line up. For residents, that can create cash-flow pressure even when income is sufficient over the course of the month. For multifamily operators, the same timing mismatch can lead to payment questions, late-payment follow-up, and additional administrative work.
