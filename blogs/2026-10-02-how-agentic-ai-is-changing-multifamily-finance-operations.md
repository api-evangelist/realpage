Multifamily accounting and finance teams manage high volumes of invoices, reconciliations, recurring expenses, accruals, and reporting tasks. Agentic AI can support defined, multi-step accounting workflows by handling routine preparation, monitoring expected activity, and surfacing exceptions for finance staff to review. The practical shift is from processing every transaction manually to focusing staff time on exceptions, controls, and decisions that require accounting judgment.
