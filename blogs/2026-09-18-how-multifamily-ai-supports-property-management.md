Multifamily AI, the strategic use of AI in property management, has evolved beyond the early days of chatbots and isolated automation. Today, AI systems can support prospect and resident engagement, surface operational insights, and assist with repeatable workflows across property management. The opportunity is not to automate every task, but to apply AI where it can reduce friction, improve consistency, and help property teams make better-informed decisions.
