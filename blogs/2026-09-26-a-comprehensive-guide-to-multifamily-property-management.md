Multifamily property management is the coordinated oversight of leasing, resident services, maintenance, accounting, compliance, and financial performance across apartment communities. As portfolios become more centralized and operational workflows more connected, property management increasingly depends on consistent processes, accurate data, and technology that gives teams visibility across the resident lifecycle. For operators, the challenge is not simply managing more tasks.
