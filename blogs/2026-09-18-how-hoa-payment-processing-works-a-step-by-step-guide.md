HOA payment processing is the end-to-end workflow that moves an assessment from billing through payment authorization, settlement, ledger posting and reconciliation. An assessment must be billed to the right homeowner, moved through the selected payment channel, posted to the correct ledger and matched to the association’s bank deposit. The operational burden appears when any part of that chain is disconnected.
