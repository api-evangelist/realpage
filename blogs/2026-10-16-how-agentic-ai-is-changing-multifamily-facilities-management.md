Multifamily maintenance delays rarely come from one task. They build across service request intake, technician assignments, inspections, unit turns, vendor scheduling, purchasing, and follow-up. Agentic AI can help coordinate those connected workflows so property teams spend less time moving work from one step to the next and more time completing the maintenance that keeps apartment homes ready and residents supported.
